Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Short version: your images never leave your device.

Your images and projects

LineGap works inside your browser. Pictures you open, your projects and your stickers are stored only on your device (in the browser's storage). They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

License check

When you activate a key, LineGap sends the key (and, for Lemon Squeezy keys, a device label such as "LineGap · iPad") to the shop you bought it from, Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy, to confirm it is valid. About once a week the app checks again. No images or project data are sent.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy. We never see your card details. The shop shares your order email with us so we can help you with your purchase. Their own privacy policies apply to the checkout.

No tracking

LineGap has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (like any website host).

Deleting your data

To remove everything, delete your projects in the app, or clear this website's data in your browser settings. To have your purchase email removed from our records, email us.
